Questions

Honest answers,
no fine print.

Is Typple really free?

The core flow — scan, split, settle — is free forever. Power features (export, multi-currency trips, AI summaries) live in an optional Pro subscription. No bank linking required at any tier.

Do I need to link my bank account?

No. Typple is a calculator and coordinator — not a wallet. We hand off payment to whatever your group already uses (Venmo, Cash App, PayPal, Revolut, IBAN, even cash).

How accurate is the receipt scanning?

Typple uses Gemini's vision model, which currently parses around 98% of typical restaurant receipts correctly on the first try. When it's unsure, items appear with a confidence flag you can tap to confirm or edit.

Can I use it with friends who don't have Typple?

Yes. Anyone can open a settlement link in their browser, see their share, and pay — no install required. Friends who do install get a richer history.

Where is my data stored?

Receipts are processed in-region (EU or US) and deleted from our servers within 30 days. Only the parsed line items stay attached to your bill history. Read more in our privacy policy.
